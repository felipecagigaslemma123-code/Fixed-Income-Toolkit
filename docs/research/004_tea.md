# Objective

The objective of this research is to validate and record the methodology used
to calculate the **Effective Annual Rate (TEA)** for Argentine Treasury
capitalizable instruments in the production pricing engine.

Its purpose is to identify the correct compound annualization methodology,
validate the applicable market conventions, compare official and bibliographic
sources, and document the rationale supporting the approved implementation.

Unlike the Nominal Annual Rate (TNA), the TEA incorporates the effect of compounding and therefore represents the effective annual growth rate of the investment.

# Financial Context

The Effective Annual Rate (TEA) expresses the Holding Period Return as an annual compounded return.

Unlike the Nominal Annual Rate (TNA), the TEA assumes reinvestment of returns during the annualization process.

Its primary purpose is to measure the effective annual performance of an investment while accounting for compound growth.

Because TEA is derived from HPR, the validity of the calculation depends directly on the correctness of:

- the Holding Period Return;
- the day-count convention;
- the annualization methodology.

Observed conclusion:

The workbook assumes a compound annualization methodology using a 360-day basis.

This observation will be validated against:

- Official Treasury documentation.
- IAMC methodology.
- Bibliographic references.

before being approved for production implementation.

## Compound-equivalence evidence

Bodie, Kane and Marcus define the effective annual rate as the annual rate that
compounds to the same terminal value as the total holding-period return. Their
general relationship is:

```text
1 + EAR = (1 + holding_period_return)^(1 / T)
```

where `T` is the holding period expressed in years. The reference workbook
historically substitutes `T = Days30/360 / 360`, which gives:

```text
TEA = (1 + HPR)^(360 / Days30/360) - 1
```

The equivalent effective monthly rate under the same conventional basis is:

```text
TEM = (1 + HPR)^(30 / Days30/360) - 1
```

This is compound equivalence, not simple proportional annualization. For the
approved production yield convention, the time fraction is instead
`T = actual_residual_days / 365`.

## Calendar-month question

The Secretaría de Finanzas defines contractual LECAP capitalization and VPV on
a 30/360 basis with conventional months. That convention remains approved for
VPV. Secondary-market residual yields are a distinct calculation and use actual
calendar days on a 365-day basis under the IAMC-reconciled decision.

## A3 Academia — Interest Rates

**Status**

Reviewed.

**Evidence collected**

Page 5 of `Tasas-de-interes-y-medidas-de-sensibilidad-2025.pdf` gives the
discrete-compounding relationship `FV = C × (1 + R/m)^(mT)` and the continuous

aligned with payment frequency.

**Conclusion**

This independently supports compound rather than proportional transformation.
It does not define the Argentine market label TEA or replace the instrument's
documented 30/360 time convention.

## IAMC

**Status**

Validated against the official IAMC fixed-income methodology glossary on
11 August 2026.

**Items to verify**

- TEA methodology.
- Relationship between HPR and TEA.
- Compound annualization procedure.
- Official day-count convention.

**Evidence collected**

The IAMC report reconciliation establishes compound annualization of the period
return for TEA using actual residual days and defines TEM as the monthly
compound-equivalent rate:

```text
TEA = (1 + HPR)^(365 / actual_residual_days) - 1
TEM = (1 + TEA)^(1 / 12) - 1
```

The second expression preserves monthly compound equivalence after the ACT/365
annualization.

**Conclusion**

The approved compound-equivalence formulas are supported by the official IAMC
methodology. A current-rate display additionally requires a contemporaneous A3
price; an IAMC daily snapshot is not relabeled as current.

# Final Decision

## Validation Status

🟢 Validated against official IAMC glossary (owner decision, 2026-08-11)

## Approved Methodology

Compound equivalence between holding-period return and a common-period
effective rate. Production use requires the documented, date-aligned input
contract.

## Approved Formula (corrected 2026-08-11 after empirical IAMC reconciliation)

```text
residual_days = actual calendar days from settlement to maturity
TEA (TIREA)   = (1 + HPR) ** (365 / residual_days) - 1
TEM           = (1 + TEA) ** (1 / 12) - 1
```

**Correction note.** The earlier 30/360, 360-basis form did not reproduce the
published IAMC TIREA. The secondary-market effective yield uses **actual days on
a 365-day basis (ACT/365)**, verified against the BYMA/IAMC daily report for
S16O6 (10-Aug-2026): 66 actual days, TEA≈27.9% vs published 27.97%. The 30/360
convention remains correct **only** for the maturity value (VPV, issue→maturity,
R-001).

The owner reconfirmed this split convention on 12 August 2026.

## Approved Market Convention

ACT/365 (actual calendar days, 365-day basis) for the secondary-market effective
yield, reconciled against the official IAMC daily report.
