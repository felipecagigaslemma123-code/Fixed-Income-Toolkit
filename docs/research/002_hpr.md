# Objective

The objective of this research is to validate the methodology used to calculate the **Holding Period Return (HPR)** for Argentine Treasury capitalizable instruments before implementing the production pricing engine.

This document does **not** define the production implementation.

Its purpose is to collect evidence, compare methodologies, validate assumptions, and document the rationale supporting the future implementation.

# Financial Context

The Holding Period Return (HPR) measures the effective return obtained by purchasing an instrument at the market price and holding it until maturity.

For capitalizable Treasury instruments, the investor purchases the instrument below its maturity value and receives the full maturity value at redemption.

HPR therefore measures the realized return over the remaining investment period.

# Excel Observation

Observed worksheet:

`Lecap y Boncap`

Observed methodology:

$begin:math:display$
HPR\=\\frac\{VPV\}\{Market\\ Price\}\-1
$end:math:display$

Where:

- VPV = Maturity Value per VN 100
- Market Price = Current purchase price per VN 100

Current status:

Observed only.

Retained by project-owner decision on 5 August 2026. External market-source
validation and the market-price input contract remain pending.

The additional bibliography reviewed on 6 August 2026 did not establish a new
Argentine market-specific HPR definition. This absence of additional evidence

# Final Decision

Validation Status

🟢 Validated (owner decision, 2026-08-10)

Approved Methodology

Holding Period Return over the residual period, using the maturity value (VPV,
R-001) as the numerator and the secondary-market price as the denominator.

Approved Formula

`HPR = VPV / Market Price - 1`

Approved Market Convention

Market price is the A3 secondary-market price at the 24h settlement term
(`plazo` 001). VPV per R-001.

Primary Reference

Reference workbook `Lecap y Boncap`; owner decision 2026-08-10.

Implementation Approved

Yes — implemented in `src/financial_engine/letras_yield.py`; regression tests in
`tests/test_letras_yield.py`. External IAMC/official cross-validation remains a
recommended follow-up but is not a blocker per the owner decision.
